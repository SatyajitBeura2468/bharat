# Concept-generation manifest

## Mode

The eleven visual concepts were produced with the built-in image-generation tool in `ui-mockup` mode. The navigation/hero plate established the style anchor; subsequent plates referenced that image for palette, typography, brass linework and institutional tone while using independent layouts.

Final files are saved in `docs/concepts/plates/`.

## Shared prompt constitution

Every plate used this common direction:

> Build a high-resolution, shippable web-interface concept for BHARAT, an independent cinematic evidence-led digital atlas of India. Use the design philosophy “ancient depth, modern precision”: Midnight Ink, Warm Ivory, restrained Aged Brass, Peacock Teal, Sandstone, Forest and Lotus Crimson; monumental editorial serif, precise sans-serif, fine cartographic and scientific linework, generous whitespace and calm cinematic depth. Avoid government implication, State Emblem, patriotic filler, fake statistics, fake scripts, copyrighted media, bento grids, repetitive rounded cards, excessive glow, blur, glassmorphism, neon and watermarks. Keep all visible controls intentional and usable.

## Plate-specific prompts

1. **Navigation and hero** — full-viewport topographic terrain, minimal navigation, BHARAT/भारत identity, brand line, supporting sentence, two working-action concepts and independent-project disclaimer.
2. **Interactive map** — dominant cartographic field, state/capital layer, Odisha selection, search, layers, controls, editorial rail, textual state fallback and state-detail action.
3. **History timeline** — horizontal museum chronology, mobile vertical adaptation, evidence/approximate/debated markers, expanded era and source categories.
4. **Living fabric** — asymmetric culture tapestry spanning architecture, dance, textiles, literature material, cuisine, crafts, languages, music, festivals and Indigenous/tribal cultures.
5. **Nature journey** — panoramic geographic transect with chapter rail, terrain/elevation language, rivers, wetlands, forests, source/method and static fallback.
6. **Innovation** — ancient astronomical measurement line transforming into orbital geometry and a modern scientific interface; discipline chapter index and sourced-story affordance.
7. **India in data** — dominant accessible chart, supporting comparisons, metadata rail, keyboard summary and table alternative; only `SAMPLE STRUCTURE` labels, no numbers.
8. **Stories and search** — editorial scrollytelling collection under an open, keyboard-operable command palette with categories, previews and recent queries.
9. **Footer and sources** — closing invitation, open source/methodology treatment, neutral registry fields, independence disclaimer, accessibility, language and theme controls.
10. **Odisha state page** — state-specific sandstone/coastal atmosphere, reviewed regional-script name, terrain silhouette, chapter rail, textual map alternative and source metadata.
11. **Mobile homepage** — mobile-first hero, touch map and bottom sheet, vertical timeline, culture seam, full-screen search/menu, safe areas and 44px controls.

## Safety repair prompts

Targeted edits changed only unsafe generated details:

- removed emblem-like and flag-like timeline imagery;
- replaced artifact pseudo-script with script-free geometry and material;
- replaced culture and story pseudo-manuscript imagery with blank ruled folios;
- removed stray pseudo-Devanagari from the innovation plate;
- replaced fabricated source IDs, dates, methods and licences with neutral field labels;
- corrected the Odisha regional-script treatment and removed a fabricated review date.

The concepts remain layout references. Production map geometry, facts, dates, source records, programmes and media require independent validation.
